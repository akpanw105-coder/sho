## Brand & Style

This design system embodies refined dark luxury, high-fashion editorial restraint, and tactile modern commerce. Designed for an elevated footwear boutique, it communicates exclusivity, intentional craft, and curated taste.

The aesthetic fuses **Editorial Minimalism** with **Atmospheric Layering**:
- **Tone:** Discerning, contemplative, timeless, and sophisticated.
- **Visual Personality:** Deep pine-black canvases paired with luminous ivory serifs, architectural hairline borders, soft tactile inputs, and muted stone-taupe accents.
- **Imagery Context:** Rich photography treated with cinematic shadow depth, natural grain, and controlled directional lighting to highlight leather grains, textures, and bespoke footwear silhouettes.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immersive nocturnal atmosphere using deep forest undertones rather than stark synthetic blacks, yielding organic warmth and depth.

### Surface Architecture
- **Canvas Base:** `#061310` — Pure abyssal pine-black for full-screen depth.
- **Card Tier 1:** `#121C19` — Primary container surface for product showcases, sheets, and grouped data.
- **Card Tier 2 / Elevated:** `#162420` — Interactive nested states, input fields, and pill backgrounds.
- **Hairline Structural Borders:** `#1F302B` (standard container outlines) and `#263832` (interactive focus borders).

### Text & Accents
- **Primary Editorial Text:** `#F4F0E8` — Warm ivory providing high legibility against nocturnal surfaces without clinical harshness.
- **Secondary / Supporting Text:** `#A7B0AA` — Muted olive-tinted silver for metadata, subtitles, labels, and icons.
- **Accent & Primary Action:** `#EAE3D2` — Warm alabaster cream for primary CTA fills; hovering transitions to `#D8CEBD` or muted taupe `#B7A696`.
- **Text on Accent Buttons:** `#061310` — Heavy contrast against stone and cream buttons for instant tap clarity.

## Typography

The typography pairing contrasts a classical editorial serif display with an engineered, geometric sans-serif for UI utility:

1. **Display & Editorial Narrative (Playfair Display):** Used for brand logos, hero campaign statements, product showcase titles, and large card headings. It grounds the product in bespoke fashion craftsmanship.
2. **System Interface & Functional Content (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Delivers clarity across filters, pricing, size pickers, body descriptors, and checkout flows.
3. **Hierarchy Rules:** Large editorial headings should never compete with technical data. Maintain tight line-heights on serifs for a compact editorial magazine layout, while allowing comfortable breathing space for sans-serif microcopy.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout structure is built around high-density editorial storytelling alternating with generous negative space.

### Grid Systems
- **Mobile (Phone):** 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` screen margins and `0.75rem` to `1rem` gutters. Product lists utilize 2-column asymmetric cards.
- **Tablet / Small Desktop:** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins.
- **Desktop:** 12-column fixed grid with a max width of `1280px`, centered, using `1.5rem` gutters and `3rem` outer margins.

### Spacing Cadence
- Vertical pacing relies on `space-lg` (24px) between grouped card elements and `space-xl` / `space-2xl` between editorial storytelling chapters.
- Product cards maintain an internal padding of `space-md` (16px) with intentional whitespace at the bottom to frame pricing cleanly.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated via tonal stratification, fine perimeter hairbars, and selective glassmorphism rather than muddy drop shadows.

### Surface Tiers
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#061310` base canvas.
- **Level 1 (Structural Containers):** `#121C19` surface with a 1px solid border of `#1F302B`. No shadow.
- **Level 2 (Floating Modals & Sheets):** `#162420` surface with a 1px border of `#263832`, accompanied by an ambient diffused shadow: `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Level 3 (Navigation / Floating Controls):** Glassmorphic surfaces with `background: rgba(18, 28, 25, 0.75)` and `backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(140%)`, edged with a 1px top border of `rgba(244, 240, 232, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Pill-Shaped (Level 3)** geometry balanced against crisp structural rectangular cards:

- **Buttons, Badges, Search Bars, and Inputs:** Full pill styling (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to soften the dark, masculine color palette and provide distinct hit targets.
- **Cards & Modals:** Generously rounded corners (`rounded-xl` or 20px–24px) that feel like architectural slate tiles.
- **Interactive Thumbnails & Swatches:** Circular radius for color swatches and squircle (`rounded-lg` / 12px) for thumbnail previews.

## Components

### Buttons & Call-to-Actions
- **Primary CTA:** Full pill shape, solid `#EAE3D2` fill, `#061310` label text, `font-weight: 600`. Hover state transitions seamlessly to `#D8CEBD`.
- **Secondary / Outlined:** Full pill shape, transparent background, 1px outline of `#263832`, `#F4F0E8` text. Hover state shifts to `#162420` background.
- **Icon Actions:** 44px circular pills with `#162420` fill, 1px border of `#1F302B`, and `18px` crisp vector stroke in `#F4F0E8`.

### Product Cards
- **Architecture:** Contained within `#121C19` background cards with subtle `#1F302B` border.
- **Imagery Area:** Photography is set against slightly desaturated warm-neutral studio backdrops with floating circular favorite icons in the upper-right corner.
- **Typography Block:** Left-aligned product name in `body-md` semibold, followed by category in `body-sm` (`#A7B0AA`), with price prominently stated in bold ivory.

### Size & Attribute Pickers
- **Pill Grid:** Individual size choices rendered as circular or pill badges (`#162420` fill, 1px `#1F302B` border).
- **Selected State:** Solid ivory `#EAE3D2` background with `#061310` text, communicating immediate tactile confirmation.
- **Disabled State:** Opacity 35% with diagonal strike-through hairline.

### Floating Bottom Navigation
- **Dock Metaphor:** Compact floating pill floating 16px above screen bottom.
- **Materiality:** Deep green glassmorphism (`rgba(18, 28, 25, 0.85)`, `blur(16px)`), bordered by a 1px boundary of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Iconography:** 20px minimalist stroke icons in `#A7B0AA`, with the active tab illuminated in `#EAE3D2` and signaled by a subtle 3px dot indicator.

### Input Fields & Search
- **Container:** Height of 52px, full pill shape, background `#121C19`, 1px border `#1F302B`.
- **Placeholder:** `#A7B0AA` font, paired with clean minimalist vector search or field icons.
- **Focus:** 1px border transitions smoothly to `#B7A696`.
